# Story and reading path

Develop the actual sequence: problem, concrete example, useful idea, what the reader can try and the next appropriate action. A handbook and a promotional post may be different projects. Keep the reading path usable without narration or auto-advance.

Put the proposed page/scene plan in NNN-review.md before implementation, including what the reader sees and does on each page. After saved answers arrive, preserve the confirmed sequence in docs/projects/ID/versions/VVERSION/story.md and link to decisions.md. Put new unresolved choices in NNN-review.md and wait for file answers; never ask them in chat or a question widget. Record readiness separately from acceptance; do not require a full reread of unchanged material.
