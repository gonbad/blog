# Issue 001 — direct seeking marked skipped sections as visited

Observed during the actual first-post browser rehearsal: with normal motion enabled, jumping from section 1 directly to section 3 colored section 2 as visited.

Cause: the progress updater treated intermediate frames of smooth scrolling as normal reading positions. The same check with reduced motion alone did not expose the problem.

Correction: direct navigation records its target and ignores intervening scroll positions. Wheel/touch interaction cancels that suppression so ordinary reading can update progress normally.

Verification: the browser acceptance script now performs direct seeking with normal motion and checks the skipped section. It then exercises reduced-motion navigation and the offline target.

Scope: this post's navigation helper and the publishing recipe's browser review. Moom lesson: verify the semantic claim made by a progress indicator, and exercise both normal and reduced-motion routes when they behave differently. No timing benchmark is needed.
